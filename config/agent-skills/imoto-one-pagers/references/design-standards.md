# Design Standards

## Format

- Default to A4 portrait: `@page { size: A4 portrait; margin: 0; }`.
- Keep the final output to exactly one page.
- Use HTML/CSS rendered through Chrome; do not export final PDFs directly from markdown or a word processor.
- Use large readable text. Do not solve fit problems by making body copy tiny.

## Visual Style

- Clean, sparse-to-medium density, technology-forward, operator-friendly.
- Use Imoto green as an accent with neutral backgrounds.
- Avoid one-note palettes, generic AI graphics, stock photos, clip art, and decorative visuals that do not explain the offer.
- Driver Shield may use a distinct dark treatment; Route Optimization and Predictive Maintenance should feel demo/audit/data-readiness oriented.

## Layout

- Vary layouts between one-pagers so they do not look like identical templates.
- Keep the main visual large enough to inspect.
- For route one-pagers, do not use a tiny standalone graph if labels become unreadable; prefer a wider audit-breakdown visual with timing, route, or constraint context.
- Place proof boundary and CTA in clear boxes near the bottom or side; do not hide proof boundary copy in a footnote.
- Use three value cards when possible, with concise headings and short evidence-based body copy.
- Crop screenshots to the useful screen area. Remove browser chrome, app headers, blank margins, and lower text fragments when they are not informative.

## Final Checks

- Text stays inside containers.
- Nothing overlaps.
- Preview PNG and PDF render match.
- The page can be understood in 30 seconds.
- The final PDF looks like a professional sell sheet, not a school report.
